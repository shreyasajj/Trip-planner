📱 How to send your location from Android
  1. Install OwnTracks from the Play Store (free, open source).
  2. In OwnTracks → Preferences → Connection: set Mode to MQTT, host to your Mosquitto broker's address, and your broker username/password.
  3. Set Identification → Username to your name exactly as it appears above — that's how the app knows it's you.
  4. That's it. OwnTracks publishes to owntracks/<name>/<device> and your dot appears here.
Any other publisher works too — send {"lat":37.55,"lon":126.99} to topic trip/location/<name>. No app at all? Use the 📡 Send my location button above to share straight from this browser.
