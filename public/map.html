📱 How to send your location (Android & iPhone)
  1. Install OwnTracks (free, open source — Play Store & App Store).
  2. Preferences → Connection: Mode MQTT, host = your Mosquitto broker, plus its username/password.
  3. Identification → Username = your name exactly as it appears above.
  4. Done — OwnTracks publishes to owntracks/<name>/<device> and your pin appears here.
Any other publisher works too: send {"lat":37.55,"lon":126.99} to trip/location/<name>. No app? The 📡 Send my location button streams straight from this browser.
